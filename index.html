About

I’m a Senior R&D Engineer with 5+ years of experience in mechanical design, mechatronics, robotics, and automation. I turn ideas into working prototypes by combining CAD, electronics, and C++ programming. In design, I work with SolidWorks, AutoCAD, Advance Steel, and Revit, preparing 2D and 3D drawings with GD&T, BOMs, and renders. In mechatronics, I wire and program Arduino and ESP32 controllers in C++ and integrate sensors and actuators. In robotics, I have built inspection and obstacle-detection robots, an XY plotter, and Android apps for robot control. In automation, I work with PLC basics (Siemens LOGO), inspection and maintenance automation, and ERP and DCS systems in C# and SQL. I currently work at THT-Space Electrical on product R&D and 3D-printed prototypes. I have also supported inspection and robotics-related engineering at the Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant, working under strict standards with multinational teams. I’m open to mechanical design, mechatronics, robotics, and automation roles worldwide.

My background blends:

  • Mechatronics: Arduino and ESP32 wiring, C++ programming, sensors and actuators
